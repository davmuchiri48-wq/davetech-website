Frequently askedquestions

Do I need internet?

No. Selling, receipts, stock, fees and reports all work offline. Internet is only used for the online dashboard and online backups, and they catch up by themselves when the connection comes back.

Can I use it on my phone only?

Yes. The Android app has the same features as the computer version and prints to Bluetooth receipt printers.

Which printers work?

58 mm and 80 mm thermal receipt printers, by Bluetooth or USB, and any normal Windows printer for A4 reports, statements and report cards.

What if my computer breaks or gets stolen?

The system keeps a backup every day. You can also save backups to Google Drive or a flash disk, protected with a password, and restore them on a new computer or phone.

Can I move from my current system?

Yes. Export your products, customers and sales to Excel or CSV and the system imports them. We can help with the export.

Does it send invoices to KRA eTIMS?

Not yet. Receipts show your KRA PIN and VAT, but VAT-registered businesses still need to issue eTIMS invoices separately for now.
